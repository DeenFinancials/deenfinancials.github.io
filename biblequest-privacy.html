Privacy Policy

BibleQuest: Quiz & Puzzles · Last updated: October 24, 2026

BibleQuest: Quiz & Puzzles ("the app", "we", "us") is published by DeenFinancials. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect the username and password you choose. We use them to sign you in.

Progress data. We store your game progress, such as XP and daily streak, so it is kept with your account.

Advertising and device data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location (estimated from your IP address), app interactions such as ad views and clicks, and diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.

Data stored on your device. Settings and some progress are saved locally on your device. Reminder notifications, if you turn them on, are created on your device and are not sent to our servers.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and save your progress.
  • To show ads and measure ad performance.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent fraud, and fix crashes.

3. Third-party services

The app uses services from Google. They process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (cloud database for accounts and progress): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising): policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Ads will still appear, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and progress data while your account is active. To have your account and data deleted, email us at the address below with your username, and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data sent between the app and our services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
