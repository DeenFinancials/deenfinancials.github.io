BibleQuest Account Deletion Request

Developer: DeenFinancials

How to delete your account and data:

You can delete your account directly inside the BibleQuest app by following these steps:

  1. Open the BibleQuest app and log in.
  2. Navigate to the Profile page.
  3. Click the Delete Account button at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm your choice in the popup window.

Data Retention Policy:

When you request account deletion, your account is immediately signed out and scheduled for permanent deletion.

  • Data Deleted: Your username, password, accumulated XP, and reading streaks will be permanently erased.
  • Retention Period: We retain this data for a grace period of 90 days in case you accidentally deleted your account and wish to restore it by logging back in. After 90 days, all associated data is permanently and automatically wiped from our Firebase servers.
